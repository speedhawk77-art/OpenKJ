# Optional H.264 support in the source-built candidate

The candidate does not contain Cisco's codec. Optional Cisco decoding starts disabled.

1. Open OpenKJ and choose **Help → Optional H.264 codec…**.
2. Read the complete displayed licence. Choose **Download and enable** if appropriate for your use. This one-time step needs an Internet connection and downloads directly from Cisco over HTTPS.
3. Restart OpenKJ. The verified download can then be used offline.
4. To turn it off, return to that same dialog, choose **Disable**, and restart. **Enable downloaded codec** enables the cached copy again without another download.

The dialog displays the required Cisco attribution beside the controls. Download failures or checksum mismatches do not enable the decoder. Downloads stay in your Windows user profile; administrator permission is not required. The full original binary licence is also included in the installed legal documents. Other audio/graphics functions do not require this optional download. Hardware-specific H.264 decoder availability varies by computer.

These controls implement the separate-download and user-control integration described by Cisco. They do not grant rights to perform copyrighted karaoke media or extend the licence to uses its terms exclude. The complete terms are supplied rather than replaced by this explanation.

The installer remains a draft for testing. See the consolidated remote report and final follow-up for source/build evidence and test results.
